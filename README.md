# Frozen (2013)

> "Let it go, let it go! Can't hold it back anymore."

**Frozen** is a critically acclaimed 2013 American animated musical fantasy film produced by Walt Disney Animation Studios. It tells the story of fearless Princess Anna, who sets off on an epic journey alongside a rugged iceman, his loyal reindeer, and a naive snowman to find her estranged sister Elsa, whose icy powers have inadvertently trapped their kingdom in an eternal winter.

---

### Key Information

1. **Release Date:** November 27, 2013 (United States)
2. **Directors:** Chris Buck and Jennifer Lee
3. **Main Characters:** 
   - **Elsa** - The Snow Queen with uncontrollable ice and snow powers
   - **Anna** - Elsa's optimistic and determined younger sister
   - **Kristoff** - An experienced iceman who helps Anna
   - **Olaf** - A cheerful, summer-loving snowman brought to life by Elsa
   - **Sven** - Kristoff's loyal reindeer companion

---

### Iconic Elements

- **Academy Awards:** Won two Oscars for **Best Animated Feature** and **Best Original Song** ("Let It Go").
- **Music:** Features world-famous songs composed by Kristen Anderson-Lopez and Robert Lopez.
